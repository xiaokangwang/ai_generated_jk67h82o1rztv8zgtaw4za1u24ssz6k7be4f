How to read the result

  • Strict connected: your NAT behaves like open NAT.
  • Strict failed, moderate connected: your NAT is moderate.
  • Moderate connected while strict was inconclusive: your NAT is moderate or better.
  • Neither connected: your NAT is more restrictive than moderate, or the run was inconclusive if signaling failed.
